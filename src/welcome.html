Crossposter

Allow site access

Crossposter reads the post you right-click and fills each network’s own composer. To do that it needs access to the social sites and their media servers.

    Nothing is read until you invoke Crossposter, and nothing leaves your browser. You can change site access at any time in your browser’s add-on settings.

    The prompt didn’t appear, or access is still off?
    1. Open your browser’s add-on manager (Firefox: about:addons, Chrome: chrome://extensions).
    2. Open Crossposter and choose Permissions (Firefox) or Site access (Chrome).
    3. Turn on access for every listed site, then reload any open social tabs.
